# TimeTracker – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

This policy explains what the TimeTracker mobile app ("the app") does with your data. The app is published by Team Tosty ("we", "us"). Questions: [team.tosty@proton.me](mailto:team.tosty@proton.me).

## 1. How the app works

TimeTracker is a companion app for a TimeTracker server run by (or for) your employer or organisation. When you first open the app you enter the address of that server and sign in with the account your organisation gave you. From then on, the app talks **only to that server**. We, the publisher of the app, do not receive, see or store the data you enter in the app.

Your organisation decides what happens to the data on its server and is responsible for it (in GDPR terms, it is the data controller). For questions about the data held on that server, or to exercise your rights over it, contact your organisation.

## 2. Data the app handles

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Server address | To know which server to connect to | Stored on your device |
| Email address and password | To sign in | Sent to your organisation's server. The password is not stored on the device. |
| Name, role, organisation name, language | To show your account and use your language | Received from your organisation's server, stored on your device |
| Sign-in tokens | To keep you signed in | Stored on your device, encrypted with a key that never leaves the device |
| Work hours, breaks, projects, notes, leave requests | The core purpose of the app: registering your time | Sent to and received from your organisation's server; a copy is kept on your device |
| Location (optional) | Only when you tap _"Use my location"_ while logging hours, to fill in where the work was done | See section 3 |
| App settings (reminders, app lock, language) | To remember your preferences | Stored on your device |

## 3. Location

The app asks for location permission only when you tap _"Use my location"_. It reads your position once at that moment. It never tracks your location in the background.

To turn the position into a street address, the app uses your phone's built-in geocoding service (on most Android phones this is provided by Google). The coordinates and the resulting address are saved with that time entry and sent to your organisation's server. You can always type a location yourself instead, or leave it empty.

## 4. Other permissions

- **Notifications** – to show a running work session and reminders to log your hours.
- **Biometrics** – if you turn on the app lock, your fingerprint or face is checked by your phone's operating system. The app never receives your biometric data.
- **Run at start-up** – to show a running session again after the phone restarts or the app updates.
- **Internet** – to talk to your organisation's server. All connections use HTTPS.

## 5. What the app does not do

- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
- No data is sent to us, the publisher.

## 6. Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your phone, the app's local data (such as settings and cached time entries) may be included in your phone's backup to your Google account, as for other apps. Sign-in tokens are excluded from backups.

## 7. Keeping and deleting data

Data on your device stays until you sign out, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Data on your organisation's server is kept according to your organisation's policy; your account there is created and removed by your organisation, so ask them to delete it.

## 8. Security

Connections to the server are encrypted (HTTPS only). Sign-in tokens are encrypted on the device with a key held by the operating system. You can additionally protect the app with a biometric lock.

## 9. Children

The app is intended for employees and is not directed at children under 16.

## 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data and to object to its processing. Because your data is held by your organisation, please send such requests to them. You can also contact us at [team.tosty@proton.me](mailto:team.tosty@proton.me), and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Belgium: the [Data Protection Authority](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be)).

## 11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
